VERILAB / FIELD NOTES

A circuit, a test, a waveform.

Four steps from an idea to signals you can inspect.

  1. Write the designDescribe your circuit in the left editor. This is the module you’ll simulate and synthesize.
  2. Test your logicInstantiate the design in your testbench. Apply inputs, add delays, and finish with $finish;.
  3. Run SimulationUse the button or ⌘ / Ctrl + Enter. Console shows real compiler diagnostics and $display / $monitor output.
  4. Explore the signalsIn Waveform, choose signals or click Testbench signals. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, and click to inspect values. Fit view restores the full range.
For waveforms, include $dumpfile("dump.vcd"); and $dumpvars(0, tb); in your testbench. Replace tb with your testbench module name.

Use + for supporting files. Include Verilog modules using `include "file.v". Open/Save act on their own editor; Export ZIP saves the whole project.
